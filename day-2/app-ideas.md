# Web App Ideas for Beginners

10 fun web app ideas that 12-15 year olds can build using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in under 1 hour.

## 🎮 Games & Interactive

### 1. Number Guessing Game

The computer picks a random number 1-100 and you get hints like "too high" or "too low" until you guess it.

### 2. Rock Paper Scissors Battle

Play rock-paper-scissors against the computer with a score tracker and fun animations when you win or lose.

### 3. Reaction Time Tester

A box appears at random times and you click it as fast as possible to measure your reaction speed in milliseconds.

### 4. Memory Card Matcher

Flip cards to find matching pairs with a timer and move counter to beat your best score.

### 5. Dice Roller Simulator

Roll multiple dice at once with cool animations, perfect for D&D or board games.

## 🌌 Creative & Exploration

### 6. 2D Solar System Explorer

Scroll through a side-view solar system with planets you can click to see fun facts and stats.

### 7. D&D Campaign Generator

Click a button to randomly generate quest ideas, villain names, treasure rewards, and dungeon locations for your next adventure.

### 8. Random Character Creator

Generate random game characters with stats, classes, weapons, and backstories for RPGs.

## 🛠️ Useful Tools

### 9. Countdown Timer

Set a custom countdown for gaming sessions, homework time, or challenges with sound alerts when time's up.

### 10. Pokémon Team Builder

Search Pokémon by name using the PokéAPI and build your dream team of 6 with their sprites and types displayed.

---

**Note:** All of these can be built with just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript using arrays, random numbers, click events, and basic DOM manipulation!
